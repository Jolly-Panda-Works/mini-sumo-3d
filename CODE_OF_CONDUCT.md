# Code of Conduct

Mini Sumo is a game about friendly shoving. The community around it should be the opposite: nobody gets pushed out.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and welcoming, especially to newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Respect differing viewpoints, languages and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer, **Usef Farahmand**, via the contact options on [useffarahmand.com](https://www.useffarahmand.com). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
